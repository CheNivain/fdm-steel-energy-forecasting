Five-model results

Reported final results. Lower MAE and RMSE are better; higher R² is better.

Not a fair five-way ranking yetKNN and Gradient Boosting use different test samples and high-load cutoffs. Their errors cannot establish a winner against the other three. A common test and cutoff are still required.

Why this model?

Random Forest combines 1,000 decision trees to forecast near-term energy use. It captures changing daily patterns and interactions between recent readings.

On the team's 50 kWh evaluation, it has a lower reported high-load error than Linear Regression and SVR. It is the team's deployment choice, not a proven winner over every model.

Why watch high-load error?

Errors during energy-intensive periods may matter more for operational planning. The team's priority is therefore error on intervals where actual usage is at least 50 kWh. This does not prove financial savings or capture every missed peak.

Training candidates were selected by five-fold RMSE, not by HighLoad_MAE. All four evaluation metrics remain visible.
